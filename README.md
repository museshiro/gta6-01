CALDERA VIVA: 3D WebGL sun-scorched open-world crime — 10 story jobs, Guardia Insular de Caldera with MURALLA roadblocks, ARIETE RAM/PIT units and CORMORÁN-1, crew calls, weapon wheel, garage, respect ranks, district standing, street races, day-night, radio, saves.

Isla Caldera is a fictional volcanic island: the coastal city of Puerto Brasa at scorching dusk, salt fog, black-sand beaches, a neon boardwalk and a volcano watching over it all. You are Tito Branco's new courier. Drive, steal cars, outrun the GIC, work ten story missions with Mara Vela and Tito, and become Raja Caldera.

The renderer is hand-written WebGL low-poly (no engine, no library): one shader, vertex colours, fog and an animated sea, a chase camera with an iso mode, and a top-down 2D fallback when WebGL is unavailable. The world is procedural from seed 20261124 — about 122 buildings, five districts, a pier, a harbour bridge and the volcano. Wanted 0–5 with heat that cools, a helicopter that loses your trail after 15 seconds without a crime, a four-slot weapon wheel, a garage (repairs and upgrades), localStorage saves, a pause dashboard with district standing, street races from Plaza Brasa and two synthesized WebAudio radio stations.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```
